---
name: document-changelog-entries
description: Use this skill to maintain an accurate changelog for all modifications made to the codebase.
---
- Record each fix or change in `CHANGELOG.md` under the heading '## Unreleased'.
- Use the format '- fix(<function name>): <short description>' for each entry.
- Ensure that at least three entries are documented for significant changes or bug fixes.
- Review the changelog for clarity and completeness before finalizing changes.
- Keep the changelog updated with each iteration of development to reflect the latest state of the codebase.
